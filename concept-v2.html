## 07

Method

How this release was read. No keyword rules. One language-model pass per post against a written contract, a validator on every batch, and a reviewer who re-labels flagged and sampled posts.

[ classification ]

What counted, and what did not

labels v2

Counts toward the charts

Stances from described use · cost, quota and rate-limit complaints · completed first-person switches · a reply that adds its own experience.

Shown but not scored

Recommendations and rankings without use · benchmark reposts · one-word poll answers · agreeing with someone else's experience.

Left out entirely

Pre-release speculation · vendor and reseller promotion · news and fact-checks · jokes · reply accounts writing as an assistant · anything whose target cannot be named.
